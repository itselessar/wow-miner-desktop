MINING GUIDE

Learn the stack

LESSON 01 · 2 MIN

You are running the network—not renting hash power.

WOW Miner starts the official Wownero daemon on this PC. The daemon connects to peers, downloads blocks, verifies the chain, and performs RandomWOW proof-of-work with your CPU.

Fully local

The desktop interface has no hosted backend or account system. Only `wownerod.exe` connects to Wownero peers.

LESSON 02 · 3 MIN

Use a primary WOW address.

Create or open a Wownero wallet using the official CLI wallet or a compatible wallet such as Stack Wallet. Copy the wallet's primary receiving address; it is 97 characters and begins with Wo.

  1. 1
    Open your wallet

    Use software obtained from its official source.

  2. 2
    Copy the primary address

    Do not select a subaddress—upstream mining rejects it.

  3. 3
    Paste it into Miner

    The app stores only the public address on this PC.

Never enter a seed phrase here

WOW Miner never needs your seed, spend key, wallet password, or wallet file.

LESSON 03 · 3 MIN

Verify the chain before mining.

On first run, your node downloads and validates the Wownero blockchain. The exact time depends on your connection, disk, and available peers. You can close the app and continue later.

  1. 1
    Click Start local node

    The app creates a private blockchain folder.

  2. 2
    Keep the PC awake

    Synchronization continues while the app and node are running.

  3. 3
    Wait for 100%

    The mining button unlocks when the daemon reports synchronized.

Pruned by default

A pruned node still validates the chain but stores less historical data.

LESSON 04 · 2 MIN

Choose how much CPU to commit.

Start with Balanced. Efficient leaves more CPU available; Maximum uses every detected logical thread and may increase heat and fan noise.

Watch temperatures

The app cannot control your cooling. Stop mining if the system becomes unstable or overheats.

LESSON 05 · 3 MIN

Solo means all-or-nothing blocks.

Wownero is solo-mining only. There are no pool shares or smooth daily payouts. Your CPU repeatedly attempts proof-of-work; if it finds a valid block, the full block reward is sent to your mining address.

Expected timeNetwork difficulty ÷ your hash rateThis is a statistical average, never a promise or countdown.

A block reward must mature according to Wownero's consensus rules before it becomes spendable. View it from your wallet after scanning the relevant blocks.

LESSON 06 · 2 MIN

Six checks before you press Start.

  • ✓Installer came from your trusted GitHub release.
  • ✓Release checksum matches the published checksum.
  • ✓The address is your own primary WOW address.
  • ✓No one asked for your wallet seed or private key.
  • ✓Your cooling and power settings are stable.
  • ✓The node reports synchronized with active peers.
